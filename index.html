What is the Word Unscrambler?

The Word Unscrambler turns a jumble of letters into real, playable words. Type the letters you have and it instantly lists the valid words you can spell — a fast way to beat anagram puzzles, crack a daily word game, or find the best move in Scrabble and Words With Friends.

How it works

The tool counts how many of each letter you entered, then scans a word list and keeps only entries whose letters fit within your set (a subset match). This means a word qualifies only if every letter it needs is available in the quantity you have. Results are usually grouped by length so longer, higher-scoring words are easy to spot.

Example 1: The letters LISTEN unscramble to full anagrams such as silent, tinsel, enlist, and inlets, plus shorter words like line and nest.
Example 2: The rack AEROT yields words such as orate and oater, plus useful shorter plays like rate, tear, and toe.

When to use it

Solve a stubborn anagram in a crossword or puzzle book. Find the highest-scoring word from your tiles in Scrabble or Words With Friends. Expand your vocabulary by discovering words hidden in a set of letters. Or settle a game-night dispute over whether a word is really playable.

FAQ

How does a word unscrambler work?

It rearranges the letters you enter and checks each possible combination against a dictionary, returning only the arrangements that form real words.

Can it help with Scrabble and Words With Friends?

Yes. It finds valid words from your rack of letters, which is ideal for board and tile games where you need the highest-scoring play.

Does it find shorter words too?

Yes. It returns words of many lengths that can be built from your letters, not just anagrams that use every tile.

Is the Word Unscrambler free?

Yes, it is free to use with no sign-up and works right in your browser.

Does letter order in my input matter?

No. Scrambled letters produce the same results in any order, because the tool looks only at which letters are available.
